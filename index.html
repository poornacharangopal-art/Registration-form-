Team Composition & Structure

  • Team Size: Standard competition teams usually consist of 8 pullers, though some formats allow for 6 or up to 10-12 (including substitutes).
  • substitutes: Teams may register 1-2 substitutes (squad of 10), depending on the specific event rules.
  • Gender: Many competitions feature separate men's and women's categories. Mixed categories (e.g., 4 men + 4 women) are also common, with specific rules regarding team composition, such as requiring a minimum of two women per team.
  • Role Designation: Registration forms must indicate a team captain.
  • weight Classes: Examples include Lightweight (560 kg), Middleweight (640 kg), and Heavyweight (720 kg).
